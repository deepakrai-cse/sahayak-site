    Questions people ask

    What happens to my documents and data?

    Only the person working on your order sees your files. We delete them 30 days after delivery, or sooner if you ask. We never share or sell anything you send us.

    How many revisions do I get?

    Two rounds of changes are included with every order. Small fixes after that, like a typo or a phone number, are free. Bigger rewrites are quoted before we start.

    How do I pay?

    UPI or bank transfer. For Resumes and data entry you pay after you approve the work. For event websites and event management we ask for half up front and half on delivery.

    I need it by tomorrow. Can you help?

    Often, yes. Tell us your deadline in the form and we'll reply honestly about whether we can make it. If it means working late, we tell you any extra cost before we start.
